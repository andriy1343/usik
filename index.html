Oleksandr Oleksandrovych Usyk (born January 17, 1987) is a Ukrainian professional boxer and one of the greatest fighters of his generation. Born in Simferopol, Crimea, Ukraine, he initially played football before taking up boxing at the age of 15. He later studied at the Lviv State University of Physical Culture.

As an amateur, Usyk won:

  • Gold at the 2008 European Championships
  • Gold at the 2011 World Championships
  • Gold at the 2012 London Olympic Games in the heavyweight division

Cruiserweight Title Timeline (200 lbs)

Usyk completely cleared out the 200-pound division by conquering opponents on their own home turf during the World Boxing Super Series (WBSS) tournament.

  • WBA:Won on September 17, 2016, by outpointing Krzysztof Głowacki via unanimous decision in Gdańsk, Poland.
  • WBC:Captured both simultaneously alongside The Ring magazine title on July 21, 2018. He dominated unified
  • WBO:Won on July 21, 2018, by outpointing Dillian Whyte via unanimous decision in London.

  • WBA (Super), IBF, & WBO:Won on September 25, 2021, by completely outboxing Anthony Joshua over 12 rounds in London. He defended these successfully in an August 2022 rematch, adding The Ring heavyweight title to his collection.
  • WBC:Won on May 18, 2024, by defeating Tyson Fury via split decision in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, scoring a dramatic 9th-round knockdown to complete his first undisputed heavyweight collection.
  • The Second Undisputed Heavyweight Run: After briefly giving up the IBF strap due to boxing politics/mandatory rules, Usyk knocked out Daniel Dubois in July 2025 to reclaim the IBF title and solidify himself as a two-time undisputed heavyweight champion.
